### [English]
**EatMeFirst** is a domestic pantry management and expiration tracking application designed to help you organize groceries, monitor expiry dates, and eliminate food waste while maintaining total privacy.

- **Total Privacy:** Local-First architecture where all data remains solely on your device.
- **USB Backup:** Optional continuous backup and synchronization on a USB drive.
- **Zero-Cost Scanner:** Transform an old smartphone into a barcode scanner.
- **Multilingual:** Available in English, Italian, German, French, and Spanish.
