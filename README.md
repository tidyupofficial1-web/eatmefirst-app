# EatMeFirst App 🍏📦

**EatMeFirst-App** is the core engine and client application for the EatMeFirst pantry management ecosystem. Designed with a strict **local-first** philosophy, it ensures your household inventory data remains entirely private, secure, and under your absolute control.

---

## 🚀 Core Features

- **Local-First Architecture:** All your inventory data, expiration dates, and pantry states are stored locally on your device. No mandatory cloud storage, no tracking.
- **Flexible Non-Barcode & Fractional Tracking:** Designed for real-world kitchens. Easily log items whether they come with standard barcodes, loose quantities, homemade leftovers, or fractional portions.
- **Secure USB Backup & Restore:** Easily export and import your complete pantry database via local backups (USB or local device storage) for seamless device migration or safe archiving.
- **Smart Expiration Logic:** Visual color-coded alerts to help you prioritize foods nearing their expiration date and dramatically reduce household food waste.
- **Multi-Language Persona Localization:** Built with a human-centric approach, featuring tailored guidance and tone across 5 localized languages.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- Local database engine optimized for fast, offline-first performance.
- Clean, responsive user interface designed for quick kitchen check-ins.

---

## 🔒 Privacy First

Your kitchen inventory is nobody else's business. EatMeFirst-App processes your data locally, ensuring complete data sovereignty without external server dependencies.
