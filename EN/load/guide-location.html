📍 Location and Custom Places

At first, when you start using this application, you will find all options empty, including this one[cite: 12]. We want you to create your own product destination places in the most customized way possible[cite: 12]!

You can type "Mom's freezer" or "Cellar wine shelf": simply select "Add new location" and type the name of the place[cite: 12]. That's all[cite: 12]!

💡 Avoid duplicates and confusion[cite: 12]!

The next few times, all the places you have saved will be suggested to you, and you can select them with a simple click[cite: 12]. This way, you won't have to retype the destination every time and risk creating similar variations (such as "cellar wine shelf", "cellar shelving", or "wine rack"), thus preventing a series of duplicate names referring to the same place that only create confusion[cite: 12].

(And remember: knowing exactly which room or piece of furniture a product is in also saves you from having to specify every time whether it goes in the fridge, freezer, or cupboard[cite: 12]!)
