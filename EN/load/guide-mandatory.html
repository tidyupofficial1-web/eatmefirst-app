🔒 Mandatory Item Option: What it is and how to use it

If the product you are entering has a strict and mandatory expiration date (rather than just a recommended minimum durability date), check the "🔒 Mandatory Item" box.

By checking this option, date management becomes strict: enter the exact expiration date printed on the package. For fresh products or items bought at the market without a label, rely on your experience and enter a precise date in the expiration management section.

💡 Useful Note: When the date is not mandatory or represents a simple consumption advice, you do not need to check this box: you can freely use the other expiration options (days, months, or none) further down in the form.
