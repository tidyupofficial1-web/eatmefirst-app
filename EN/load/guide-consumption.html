📊 Consumption Mode: Units or Portions

Not all foods are consumed in the same way: some are removed entirely by pieces or packs, while others are consumed gradually a little bit at a time. This option lets you choose how you prefer to manage the removal of the product[cite: 13].

Here are the two available modes[cite: 13]:

  • 📦 By Unit (Whole): This is the default choice for most products (e.g. cartons of milk, boxes of cookies, cans)[cite: 13]. When you use up this product, you will remove a whole unit at a time from your inventory[cite: 13].
  • 🧀 By Portion (10 Tenths): Ideal for foods that you cut or consume in portions (e.g. a salami, a wheel of cheese, a block of butter, or an open carton of juice)[cite: 13].
💡 How does automatic removal work?[cite: 13]

• By Portion (10 Tenths): Every time you consume a portion (e.g. 3 tenths of a wheel of cheese), you indicate the tenths consumed[cite: 13]. The app will update the remaining bar[cite: 13]. When all 10 tenths are consumed, the product will be automatically removed from the pantry[cite: 13].

• By Pack / Pieces: If you indicate, for example, 2 packs of 6 bottles (12 pieces total), every time you use items you just need to deduct the pieces consumed[cite: 13]. You won't have to manually delete rows: the app will scale inventory and automatically remove the entry only when completely emptied![cite: 13]

(If you are undecided, feel free to leave it set to "By Unit (Whole)": you can always manage standard removal at any time[cite: 13]!)
