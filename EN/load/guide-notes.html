📝 Notes and Remarks Field: How to Organize Expirations

In this space, you can enter personal reminders and notes related to the product. For example, you can write: "Reserved items for the picnic on [Date]", setting a reminder for a specific number of days before the event.

💡 Total Flexibility: This field allows you both to take simple notes (e.g., "Already opened", "Holiday stock") and to precisely schedule the use of a food item for a specific event, even if its natural expiration date is much further out in time.
