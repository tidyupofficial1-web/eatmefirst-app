🛒 Where did you buy this? (The Supplier)

This field allows you to note down which shop, supermarket, or local market you purchased the product from. It is optional, but extremely useful for keeping track of your shopping habits.

How does the app work?

• Automatic memory: Every time you type a new supplier (such as a local convenience store or a new chain), the application automatically saves it in the dropdown list (datalist). From the next time onwards, you can just start typing or select it with a single click.

📊 Powering Statistics:
Noting the source store allows the application to generate detailed statistics on where you spend the most, which retail stores you visit most often, and where you buy specific categories of fresh or long-shelf-life products!
