🏷️ Product Category: Find and Organize in a Click

What is the real purpose of dividing the pantry into categories? Not to be overly picky, but to instantly find what you need when performing a quick inventory check!

Later on, we will look at the inventory function in its specific section, which will also use other ways to list goods: for example, "Cellar wine shelf" is one of the locations you might have typed. The inventory will list everything loaded onto that shelf (where you might have placed not only wine bottles, but also corks for bottling or a demijohn). But we'll talk about that when the time comes!

How does the Category work in practice?

1. Ready-to-Use Macro-categories: It's likely that a product searched via barcode will be automatically assigned to a category (it might even be written in a language other than yours): if it looks good to you, leave it; otherwise, type in whatever category you prefer. This category will be offered alongside others already saved when you need to enter a new product. You will also find two major families ready to cover most home needs:

  • 🍎 Groceries: Dairy & Cheese, Meat & Fish, Fruit & Vegetables, Beverages, Sweets & Breakfast, Canned Goods & Preserves...
  • 🏠 Home & Personal Care: Detergents & Cleaning, Hygiene & Soaps, Paper & Disposables...

💡 2. Manage the list as you prefer:

• Remove unused ones: If a default category is not useful to you, you can select "➖ Remove category" to shorten the list and speed up selection (you can always add it back in the future!).

3. Other / Generic: Don't feel like categorizing a box on the fly because you're in a hurry? Just leave it on "Other / Generic" and move on! The important thing is to load the product; you will always have time to refine the details later.

🟢 We have finished product entry!
Check if everything looks correct, and if so, click the green button "➕ Add to Pantry" to permanently save it to memory!
