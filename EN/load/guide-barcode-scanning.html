💬 Scanning and Leo's Tips for a Perfect Connection

Once you have established the connection between your old mobile phone and your PC or laptop, this scanning feature is super simple: just click Scan and your phone automatically turns into a barcode reader (as well as a QR code reader—those weird little squares with lots of black dots... those are a kind of barcode too!).

Our goal is to have the cell phone's camera capture the code numbers. You'll see that as soon as it manages to frame all 13 numbers and the image focuses sharply, its job is done and the work instantly shifts to your PC.

Let me open a small bracket here: if you were to use a phone like the one you're holding, you could do everything on the mobile device, but it would be awkward having such a small screen to work on. So having a desktop PC or your laptop is much better!

Another detail: your PC or laptop doesn't need to be right next to the old phone, as long as it's connected to the same home Wi-Fi modem. So your phone could be in the kitchen while your PC is in the study or living room.
⚠️ Two important things to remember for Wi-Fi and security:

1. Connect devices to the main modem: Make sure both your PC and smartphone are directly hooked up to your main home Wi-Fi modem, avoiding the use of range extenders. Range extenders often create separate subnets that prevent the two devices from communicating smoothly and directly.

2. Autonomy, secure backups, and email delivery: The application uses the browser's internal memory to store your data autonomously on the device. However, to avoid any risk in case of loss or sudden failure of the phone, you can choose between two practical solutions: plugging in a dedicated USB flash drive or periodically sending the backup database via an email message. In the dedicated Utilities panel, we will explain step by step how to perform both operations simply and securely!
🔒 Ironclad Privacy:
All the data you enter stays right here with you, on the smartphone or PC you use: description, quantity, storage location, expiration date, etc. Everything remains locked down securely on your device! The only external contact made is to receive data, never to give it away.

Actually, let me clarify: we only send out one single external piece of data, which is the barcode found on the detergent box or the fish package.

Here is what your PC does: once it receives the code, it sends it to a website that has gathered massive amounts of barcodes and has created a product profile for each item containing descriptions, manufacturer, allergens, ingredients, calories, and even the product picture! If this website has that code in its archive, it sends all these details back to you for free.

And the next time you scan that code? Your PC won't even make an internet request: it already has all the data saved in its memory! You will simply need to update the quantity and the new expiration date.

💡 What if the scan yields no results?
If the code isn't listed in the websites we query, or if it's damaged, dirty, or completely missing (like with market goods), I'll let you know right away with a warning. But don't worry: we'll sort everything out brilliantly, and I'll explain how it works when you read the info on Description / Product Name!
