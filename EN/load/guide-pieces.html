📦 Items Contained in Package

This field specifies how many individual items are inside each package you are adding.

How to fill it in:

• Single / loose item: Leave it set to 1 (e.g. 1 bottle of oil, 1 bag of flour, 1 block of cheese).

• Multipack / bundle: Enter the exact number of items contained. For example:

  – 6 for a 6-bottle water pack;

  – 18 for an 18-egg carton;

  – 4 for a 4-pack yogurt.

🤖 Automatic fill:
When you scan a barcode, the app reads the online description (e.g. from Open Food Facts) and automatically sets this number for you whenever the data is available!
