📱 Smartphone Connection via QR Code

Friend, you chose to use your old smartphone. Scan the QR code below with your mobile camera:

https://192.168.1.72:8888
💡 What is the 192.168.1.72 address?

Numbers starting with 192.168... exclusively identify private home networks. That number is your PC's "internal license plate" on your home Wi-Fi: it confirms that your phone is talking only and exclusively with your computer and not with external websites on the internet.

Warnings or red text might appear in the address bar on both your laptop and phone: this is normal behavior when two devices communicate on a local network using a secure address with the "S" in https://. For safety, Windows and your browser warn you about this anomaly, but rest assured: you are always inside your own PC and secure home network.

Note: not all browsers behave the same way; some are more permissive, so the red text might not appear at all.

What to do if the blocking screen appears:
  1. Click on "Advanced" and then on "Proceed to 192.168.1.72 (unsafe)".
  2. When your phone asks for camera permission, select "Allow".
