Your in-house developer at your service

Hello friend, welcome to EatMeFirst! Your pantry data will be stored safely and securely directly inside your smartphone's browser, with no need for external servers or complex installations.

You will use your mobile phone to scan barcoded products, but you can also easily add all items that don't have one: such as items bought at market stalls or home-cooked dishes to keep in the fridge or freezer. In that case, you'll just need to fill in a few extra fields, keeping everything completely under control.

💡 A developer's tip: use your smartphone primarily to quickly load products on the fly into your app. Afterwards, you can export the database to your desktop or laptop computer to conveniently manage all other functions: checking stock reduction, browsing the shopping list, or analyzing products categorized by type or storage location. Viewing and managing these data on a larger screen makes all the difference!

Once you've made your updates from the computer, you can reload the updated data back onto your phone, ensuring that whenever you go to the supermarket, your up-to-date pantry list is right at your fingertips.

🔒 Complete Privacy and Autonomy: None of your pantry data will ever be shared or sent to third-party cloud servers. Everything stays on your local storage.
