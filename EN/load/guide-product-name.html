✍️ Description and Market Products

If you scanned a product and it was successful, you will find everything automatically filled in here. But what happens if the barcode is not recognized, is damaged, or if we are talking about products that do not have a barcode at all?

As I mentioned earlier, it often happens that a box is damaged, wet, or the code is missing from online databases (this even happens at supermarket checkouts!). Or think of all the fresh goods you buy at market stalls: onions, fruit, vegetables, or cheese from the deli counter... there are no barcodes there!

💡 No worries, your PC's memory takes care of it!

In all these cases, you will write a simple description yourself (for example "Shortbread Cookies 500g" or "Yellow Onions") and choose the category.

All the data you enter manually is saved forever on your computer. The next time you type "onions" into the box, the app will immediately suggest the entries you previously created containing that word. You will only need a single click on the matching item for your new stock (or create a new one), and everything is ready again—you just update the quantity and the new expiration date!

Couldn't be simpler!

(And if the scan didn't find the package image, don't worry: the image is not the most important thing to keep track of what you have in your pantry!)
