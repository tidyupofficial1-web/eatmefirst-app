Welcome to EatMeFirst

«Hello, we are Max and Eva, and you?

«We are a couple of regular shoppers just like you. Unfortunately, we often found expired products hidden at the back of our pantry and had to throw them away.

One day we talked about it with an IT friend to see if it was possible to create a system to better manage our home shopping. And you know how it goes... one thing led to another, we got passionate about this project and created a program capable of handling every single variable of daily shopping: from kitchen products to cleaning supplies and personal hygiene items.

Trust us, friend: what you are about to try will truly change the way you manage your groceries and home pantry.

Unlike many other applications that charge you a subscription fee or, worse, analyze and sell your data and eating habits to third parties for advertising purposes, we are proud to offer you a program that respects your home privacy. Other apps make money spying on what you cook and where you shop; we ask you only for a tiny financial contribution for support, but we give you the absolute certainty that no one will ever know what you eat or buy.

To do this, the application adopts a Local-First architecture: all your pantry data remains protected exclusively on your smartphone, while the web connection is only used to instantly fetch product details.

  • Ultra-lightweight: A pocket-sized web interface, fast and responsive on any device.
  • Maximum Privacy: Your personal purchases and consumption habits remain saved only on your device. If you don't need to load new products from outside, you can even consult your pantry offline.

How does product loading work?
You conveniently use your smartphone as a pocket scanner to scan the barcode on the package. The application connects online to free public databases only to automatically retrieve the product name, image, calories, and allergens.

All basic features of the application for your mobile phone are and will remain free forever, but I've talked too much! I'll hand over to Leo who will explain the technical aspects and also the cost.»

Leo (the developer):

«Hello friend! Max told the truth: the program saves everything automatically and in real time only in the memory of your phone, so the app is always responsive and ready to use.

Unlike many other applications that spy on you, analyze your shopping habits and sell your data to third parties or fill you with hidden fees, EatMeFirst was born with an opposite philosophy: here your data remains exclusively yours and no one will ever know what you buy or eat.

As for external backup, there are two different ways to save data: you can back up to a USB drive (to be connected to the phone via a special adapter cable to be plugged into your phone's charging port, which has a USB output on the other side to plug in the drive) or you can simply email yourself the database file from time to time. You don't need to do it with every single load (that would be a total bore!): I advise you to do it periodically after entering a few products, keeping in mind that in case of phone failure or loss, the latest unexported operations would be lost. I'll explain all the details on how to do this simply in the page dedicated to Utilities!

And now I come to the question you asked Max: how much does it cost?
All the main functions for managing groceries and pantry on your mobile phone will remain free for ever. As for external backup support and data transfer services, you can try them freely for a few free months; afterwards, the service will require a tiny financial contribution (we almost feel ashamed to ask!): we are talking about 20 Euros for 6 months or 35 Euros for the annual subscription. You will find all details and options in the app menu by clicking on Subscription.

If you decide not to activate it, nothing dramatic will happen: the database on your phone will continue to work perfectly forever, you can continue loading and unloading products and shopping without problems, the only thing that will deactivate is assistance for backing up to external media.»

Let's start!
