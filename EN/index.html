Welcome to EatMeFirst

«Hi, we are Max and Eva, and you?

«We are a couple of regular consumers who, just like you, go grocery shopping every day. Unfortunately, it happened often to us too to find expired products at the back of the pantry and have to throw them away.

One day we talked to a software developer friend to see if it was possible to create a system to better manage home purchases. And you know how it goes... one thing led to another! We got passionate about this project and created a program capable of considering every single variable of daily shopping: from kitchen products to detergents and personal care items.

Believe us, friend: what you are about to try will truly change the way you manage your home groceries and pantry. Leo, our developer friend, inspired by our continuous requests, worked absolute magic!

One of the first essential things we asked him for was maximum privacy: we didn't want our buying habit data ending up on external servers to be sold to advertising companies. That’s why we decided that everything will stay exclusively on your device (desktop PC or laptop).

  • Lightweight: You won't need to install Gigabytes of files, just a few hundred Megabytes.
  • Autonomous: It won't slow down your computer and you can use it even without an Internet connection once your usual products are saved.

How does loading products work?
Your PC works locally, but when you need to load a product, it uses the barcode on the package. The program connects to public databases to retrieve details, calories, allergens, and even product images.

And to read the code? Instead of making you buy a supermarket laser scanner (which costs around €15), we asked Leo for a zero-cost alternative. The result? You just need an old smartphone without a SIM card, connected to your home Wi-Fi, to turn it into a professional scanner. Cost: 0 Euros!

In short: complete management of barcode products (including those from local markets!), total privacy, and zero-cost hardware.

You might be asking yourself: «So how much does all this cost me?»
It might sound strange, but it's a gift. No subscriptions, no ads, no tricks: it's free and always will be.

One last question might be on your mind: «How can I trust a downloaded program? What if it contains viruses?»
We guarantee 100% safety, but we want you to feel completely reassured. I'll pass the word to Leo, who will explain how to run a direct check with your antivirus.

Leo (the developer):

«Hi friend! As a developer, I completely understand caution when launching software for the first time. Windows Defender or your trusted antivirus (like Avast, Norton, or Bitdefender) might show a warning simply because EatMeFirst is independent software developed by us and not by a multinational corporation.

How to run a scan (it's super easy):
All you need to do is right-click on the compressed or extracted folder of the program with your mouse and select 'Scan with Windows Defender' (or your antivirus name, e.g., Scan with Avast). In just a few seconds, your antivirus will check everything and confirm that the file is clean.

Why might Windows or browser warnings appear?

Now, since we'll conveniently use your old smartphone as a barcode scanner, you will need to "pair" the phone with your computer. Windows is great for security, but sometimes overreacts: when it sees two devices communicating locally, it might display warnings like 'unsecure website' or 'connection not private'.

Don't be alarmed: you are connecting YOUR personal phone, not a stranger's! Since your antivirus already verified the program is 100% clean, it's just the computer being extra cautious.

How do we connect the phone?

  • Via Wi-Fi: If you choose wireless, make sure both your PC and phone are connected directly to your main home router and not a Wi-Fi extender, as extenders can act like a wall preventing devices from discovering each other.
  • Via USB Cable: If you want maximum stability and to avoid network hiccups, you can connect the phone directly via USB cable! It's a very convenient option that bypasses network issues.

Of course, you could also type barcodes manually on your keyboard, but entering 13 digits for every single can or jar isn't very fun! Alternatively, you can use a classic laser barcode gun, but using your smartphone (via Wi-Fi or USB) remains the most practical free solution.

I'll guide you through all the pairing steps calmly as soon as you enter the Product Loading section.

Now everything is ready to begin: whenever you're ready, let's start!»
