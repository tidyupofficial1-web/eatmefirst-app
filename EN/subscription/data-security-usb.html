Data Protection and Security on USB Drive

EatMeFirst was created with a very specific philosophy: total privacy and absolute control over your data. Unlike traditional cloud-based software (where data ends up on unknown servers on the internet), EatMeFirst runs entirely and directly on your device.

Why is external backup important?

Working locally is fantastic for privacy, but it exposes you to physical hardware risks associated with the devices we use every day. Consider these unfortunate but far from rare events:

  • Sudden power surges or voltage spikes that can damage your computer's power supply or motherboard.
  • Sudden failure of the Hard Drive or SSD where your working files are stored.
  • Irreparable PC failures or loss of the device.

How does the subscription help?

The subscription unlocks the advanced mirroring and instant backup feature to a USB drive or external storage connected to your PC. This way, every modification, addition, or removal of products is mirrored in real time onto the USB drive. If your computer stops working, you simply need to reinstall the application on a new PC, plug in the USB drive, and pick up right where you left off, without losing a single piece of data!

Our promise of freedom

Even if you decide not to subscribe, EatMeFirst will continue to work forever on your PC by saving data locally. The subscription is simply an additional security tool for those who manage large pantry inventories and want complete peace of mind.
