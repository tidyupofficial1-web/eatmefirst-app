📌 Guide: To-Do List

This section is designed as a dynamic reminder for errands to run outside the home, particularly useful when you are already out grocery shopping or managing daily tasks.

Instead of chores or household cleaning, use this list to quickly jot down tasks such as:

  • Hardware Store: Getting keys copied or buying small hardware items.
  • Bookstore / Stationery: Picking up school books or office supplies.
  • Pharmacy: Buying prescribed medications or over-the-counter products.
  • Various Errands: Dropping off or picking up clothes at the dry cleaner, making payments, or collecting packages.

Each added item can be checked off once completed or deleted permanently from the list.
