How the Smart Shopping List Works

The EatMeFirst Shopping List feature is designed to guide you step by step down supermarket aisles, saving you time while preventing duplicate purchases or forgotten items.

✍️ 1. From manual entry to automation
When you first use the app and your pantry is still empty, build your list by manually adding the items you need (just like a piece of paper). As you load your pantry, the list will start creating itself: whenever a food item runs out completely, it automatically reappears on the list to be re-purchased!
🧠 2. Predictive stock calculation
The app doesn't wait for a product to run out! It calculates based on your consumption pace: if it detects that an item still has pieces available, but they won't last until your next shopping trip, it warns you by adding it to the list and clearly showing the remaining units left at home.
📲 3. Real-time checklist at the supermarket
All products are sorted by grocery category / aisle, so you'll never have to run back and forth across the store. As you pick up an item from the shelves and put it in your cart, tap it on the screen: the text will be crossed out and automatically move to the bottom of the list, leaving only what you still need to get right at the top!
🎨 The 3 Colors of the Shopping List:
🔴 Red All gone: Product is completely out of stock in the pantry.
🟢 Green Running low: Product is available at home but with insufficient pieces until the next shopping trip.
🩶 Gray New Item: Note manually entered by the user not yet present in the database.

💡 Practical tips for management and synchronization:

Remember that from your computer (desktop or laptop) you can comfortably manage your daily operations: record the usage of consumed items, check various app features, and add new items to the shopping list.

A quick tip: if you want to log a product with a barcode and verify the automatic information import from the online database, you can easily do it directly from your PC by typing the barcode manually (without needing to connect your smartphone). If there are only a few items, typing them manually is very fast, but for a large batch of groceries, using your smartphone as a quick scanner is much more convenient!

Before leaving home: don't forget to transfer the updated database from your computer to your mobile phone. You can do this easily using a dedicated USB flash drive or by sending yourself a handy email message with the backup file attached. This way, you will have everything perfectly synchronized at the supermarket!
