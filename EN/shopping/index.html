Smart Shopping Down the Aisles

Here we prepare your shopping list for when you head to the supermarket. Before leaving home, logic tells us you'll use your mobile device. Since you won't have a direct connection to your pantry while away, simply download the list to your phone by visiting the Utilities tab.

As you pick items off the shelves, tap them to check them off: they will be crossed out and automatically move to the bottom of the list!
