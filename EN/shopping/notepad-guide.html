How to use the Notepad and Camera

The Notepad & Photos section is a multimedia notebook designed to quickly capture ideas, visual reminders, and impromptu needs while you are away from home or out shopping.

💡 1. Quick notes and on-the-fly occasions
Use the Notepad to jot down any impromptu reminder. For example, while you are out you can:
  • Photograph flyers or discounts: Snap a quick photo of clothing or an item on sale to review or share with family.
  • Notes from family members: Capture a photo of a handwritten grocery list when your mother realizes you're heading to the supermarket.
  • Meetings and events: Save a memorable photo if you run into a colleague, or quickly note down an idea or recipe that came to mind.
📸 2. Integrated camera and file upload
With a single tap, you have two quick tools at your disposal:
  • 📸 Take Photo: Launch your smartphone camera or PC webcam to instantly capture receipts, notes, or price tags.
  • 📂 Upload File: Allows you to select images or documents (.pdf, .doc) already saved in your device's gallery.
📌 3. Interactive Post-it board
Every time you save a note or a photo, the app creates a colorful **Post-it** ticket on the board below. Clicking the "Open" button on an individual Post-it lets you re-read the full text or view the attached high-resolution image.
💡 Automatic synchronization:
All saved Post-its and photos are downloaded to your mobile device via the Utilities feature, so you always have them handy even when shopping offline!
