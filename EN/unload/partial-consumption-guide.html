Partial Food Consumption 📌
Eva's guide to dosing supplies

Here I wanted to be extra precise: you can manage partial product consumption by adjusting the percentage on the entire product by dragging the mouse arrow or sliding the bar along the line.


It's simple and intuitive. If you prefer not to use it, you can always remove the product entirely using the "Sold out" button and then browse visually when checking your pantry locations!
