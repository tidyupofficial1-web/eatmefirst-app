📌 Operations Log Guide: Local Consultation

The Operations Log is a consultation-only log that displays the last 30 movements (loads, unloads, notes) performed on the device in use.

  • Device Exclusive: The log operates solely on the memory of the device where the app is installed (PC or Smartphone) and is not saved to the USB stick.
  • Lightweight Shopping List: When you export or load the Shopping List onto your smartphone to go to the supermarket, the 30-operation history is not transferred, keeping the phone lightweight and focused solely on the items to buy.
