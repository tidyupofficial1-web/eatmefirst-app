📌 Backup Guide: Leo's IT Tip!

Why talk about backups?
EatMeFirst is designed to 100% protect your privacy: data doesn't go to remote servers, it stays only on your device. But precisely for this reason, if your PC or smartphone breaks down, your pantry risks disappearing with it!

🤓 Leo's Corner: "How does your Browser think?"
When you click on "Download Backup", the app generates a file called .json. By default, browsers like Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox download files **automatically** to the default system folder (usually the Downloads folder).

💡 Two smart ways to save to a USB Stick:

  • Option 1 (Recommended - The system administrator's trick):
    Go to your browser's Settings (Chrome/Edge/Firefox), look for the Downloads section, and enable the option "Ask where to save each file before downloading". This way, every time you back up, your computer's window will open and you can select **your USB stick directly** with a single click!
  • Option 2 (Dual local security):
    If you leave your browser with standard settings, the file ends up in your computer's Downloads folder. You just need to open that folder and drag the file onto the USB stick. This way you will have a **double backup copy**: one on your PC and one on your external drive.

🔑 Max's final advice:

If you use EatMeFirst on your home computer, keep the USB stick plugged in while you work. The app automatically saves every 5 minutes: keeping the drive connected ensures that your external copy is always fresh and updated with your latest changes!
