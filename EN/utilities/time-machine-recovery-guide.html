TimeMachine & Restore Points Guide

When managing product loading or unloading, mistakes or duplicate entries can happen. EatMeFirst TimeMachine protects every single operation in real-time.

🔄 1. Undo Last Operation
If you just registered an incorrect load or unload (such as a double click or quantity error), you can instantly undo the single movement with a single tap and restore the previous pantry state.
📦 2. Restore Today's Sessions (Load / Unload)
Did you get confused while recording today's shopping or consumption? This feature allows you to bulk-cancel all loads or unloads performed during the current day, returning the situation to its initial state.
🖼️ 3. Recover Deleted Images
Did you accidentally remove a product photo, receipt, or reminder from the board? TimeMachine keeps a temporary copy to let you recover them effortlessly.
💡 Total Peace of Mind:
TimeMachine works in the background on your device's memory. You will never lose control of your pantry, even during small everyday slip-ups!
