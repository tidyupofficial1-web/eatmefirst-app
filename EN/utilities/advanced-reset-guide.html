📌 Advanced Reset Guide: Fresh Start vs Total Reset

Managing your pantry requires flexibility. Under Utilities, you will find two targeted reset options:

1. Fresh Start (Clear Quantities): Clears stock levels for all products while keeping database records intact (names, categories, brands, photos, and stores). This is the ideal choice for creating a new household inventory without having to rewrite the catalog from scratch.

2. Total Reset: Permanently deletes all data, history, and settings, returning the application to its initial first-install state.
