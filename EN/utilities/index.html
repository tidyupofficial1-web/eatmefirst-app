Utilities & Security
Your pantry's safety, protected on your device, USB stick, or via e-mail! 🛡️💾

Remember a fundamental detail: your smartphone primarily serves as a fast and smart scanner. By using it to scan barcodes, the app instantly queries online databases and automatically fills in product details (description, images, ingredients, and allergens), saving you time and avoiding manual typing.

You can also perform this operation from a PC by manually entering the 13-digit barcode: an excellent solution for registering occasional items, but for full grocery loading, the smartphone remains the most convenient and fastest option!

To ensure absolute privacy, EatMeFirst works in Local-First mode: all your data is saved in real-time on the internal memory of the device in use (PC or mobile).

💾 Backups, USB Stick & Leo's Study Center

1. 🖥️ PC/Laptop at home, 📱 Smartphone on the go

We recommend using the application on a PC or laptop for daily management and consulting your pantry at home. On your smartphone, you can transfer the updated database before going out, so you have your shopping under control in the supermarket aisles.

2. 💾 USB Stick Backup (from PC) or E-mail Transfer

Data is permanently stored in the browser you use. When working on a desktop or laptop PC, connecting a dedicated USB stick causes the program to automatically archive a simultaneous backup copy. If you use your smartphone instead, the simplest and most immediate method to move or send the updated database is via an e-mail message.

Both the USB stick and e-mail guarantee three fundamental advantages:

  • Data transport: easily move your pantry database between your PC and phone.
  • Backup copy: always stay safe from unexpected computer or phone failures.
  • Shopping synchronization: keep your shopping list perfectly updated before leaving home.
