By connecting a USB stick, saving occurs simultaneously on both the device and the USB stick: this way, even in the event of a PC or smartphone failure or breakage, your data remains safe on the stick and ready to be used on another device.

Furthermore, inside the app, TimeMachine records every modification in real-time to let you correct mistakes, cancel sessions, or make a "Fresh Start" with complete peace of mind. Explore the sections below and use the information Post-its for any guide!
