📌 Quick Guide: Transparent and Automatic Updates

You don't need to worry about doing anything! Program updates require no manual action because they are managed entirely on our server.

  • No manual downloads: You don't need to download files or install packages on your device; the app connects directly through our server to fetch improvements.
  • New features: If there are updates, fixes, or new features, you will find them already ready and available online in a completely transparent way, always keeping your pantry data safe.
