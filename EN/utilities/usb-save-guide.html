📌 Save Guide: The Security of the USB Stick

In EatMeFirst, your data is **always saved in real-time to the memory of the device** you are working on (PC or smartphone). You never risk losing changes during daily use.

  • Simultaneous Saving: When you connect a USB stick, the application writes updates simultaneously to both the device and the stick.
  • Failure Protection: If your PC or smartphone suffers a sudden failure or breaks down, your data remains intact on the USB stick.
  • Immediate Portability: Just plug the USB stick into another computer to find your entire inventory ready for use.
